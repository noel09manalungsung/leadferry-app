Export your Apollo searches in three steps

SpiceStack saves the leads your Apollo search shows, including searches larger than Apollo's 2,500 limit.

  1. 1

    Pin SpiceStack

    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to SpiceStack, so it is always one click away.

  2. 2

    Open a people search

    Set your filters in Apollo as usual. Choose Net New, Total or Saved; SpiceStack exports that table.

    Open Apollo people search
  3. 3 Done

    Press Export leads

    The button sits at the bottom right of Apollo's page. Download as CSV, Excel or JSON.

Free, or with an account

Free: 100 leads a day, with or without an account. An account keeps every export so you can download it any time.

Before you start

SpiceStack pages through your Apollo search for you. Apollo's terms do not allow automated collection, and Apollo can block or close an account. You use SpiceStack at your own risk. Not affiliated with Apollo.io.

What SpiceStack collects

  • The leads and company details Apollo's search shows you, saved in this browser.
  • If you sign in, those leads are uploaded to your SpiceStack account.
  • Your email address, for signing in.
  • No browsing history, and nothing from other sites.
