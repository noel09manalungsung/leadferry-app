Export your Apollo searches in three steps

LeadFerry saves the leads your Apollo search shows, including searches larger than Apollo's 2,500 limit.

  1. 1

    Pin LeadFerry

    Click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to LeadFerry, so it is always one click away.

  2. 2

    Open a people search

    Set your filters in Apollo as usual. Choose Net New, Total or Saved; LeadFerry exports that table.

  3. 3

    Press Export leads

    The button sits at the bottom right of Apollo's page. Download as CSV, Excel or JSON.

Free, or with an account

Free: 100 leads a day, with or without an account. An account keeps every export so you can download it any time.
